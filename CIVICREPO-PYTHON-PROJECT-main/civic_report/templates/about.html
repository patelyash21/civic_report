About E-CivicHelper

E-CivicHelper is a modern smart-city civic management platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and government authorities. Our mission is to simplify civic issue reporting and create a faster, more transparent, and technology-driven communication system for urban communities.

📍

Smart Issue Reporting

Citizens can instantly report issues such as potholes, garbage overflow, water leakage, and streetlight failures using real-time location tracking and interactive maps.

⚡

Real-Time Progress Tracking

Track every complaint from submission to resolution with live progress updates, status monitoring, and transparent workflows.

🏛

Authority Management System

Government departments and municipal authorities can efficiently manage complaints, prioritize emergencies, and improve public services.

🌍

Building Smarter Cities

Our platform empowers communities to actively participate in urban development and helps create cleaner, safer, and smarter cities.
